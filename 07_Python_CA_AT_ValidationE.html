The software presented here is designed to visualize a collection of texts (poems, songs, interviews). This collection is further partitioned into text categories. Taking William Shakespeare’s 154 sonnets as our running example, the texts are classified into eight main themes—a classification accepted by several scholars of the work. This standalone program does not rely on existing CA (Correspondence Analysis) software.

The theoretical principles and basic methodology are presented in the paper: "Validation of Simultaneous Additive Trees in Textual Data Visualization" L. Lebart (JADT_2026 : 18th International Conference on Statistical Analysis of Textual Data, Palermo, Italy, 8–10 July 2026. Conference Proceedings: Editors: Antonella Plaia, Mariangela Sciandra, Alessandro Albano: . Validation of Simultaneous Additive Trees in Textual Data Visualization (2026).
